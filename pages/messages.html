National Leader's Desk

Welcome Message to Camp 2026

From: Ps. Enoch Bortey Offei (National Youth Leader) • Motto: TAC-NGYN • God First! Prophetic! Transformational!

Dear Beloved Youth & Campers,

It gives me great joy and excitement to welcome you all to this year's National Youth Camp for Central Region Zone 1 at Swedru School of Business. As your National Youth Leader, I am deeply honoured and grateful to God for bringing us together for this special gathering of fellowship, spiritual renewal, learning, and transformation.

This year's theme, “Run To Win”, drawn from 1 Corinthians 9:24–26, is a powerful reminder that the journey of life is not a casual walk but a purposeful race. The Apostle Paul encourages us to run with determination, discipline, and focus, knowing that there is an eternal prize awaiting those who remain faithful.

As young people, we must know that God has called us to run a different race — a race of faith, excellence, holiness, service, and impact. We are not running aimlessly; we are running with a clear vision and a divine purpose. The world needs us. The Church needs us. Our families need us to rise, fulfil our purpose, and finish our race well.

“Therefore, during this camp, I encourage each of you to open your hearts to God. Participate fully in every session, build meaningful relationships, discover your gifts, and allow the Holy Spirit to equip you for greater exploits. May this not be just another camp experience, but a defining moment where your destiny is shaped.”

Remember, champions are not created by chance. They are built through discipline, perseverance, sacrifice, and a determination to finish well. Let us therefore lay aside every distraction at this camp and run with endurance the race set before us.

To all participants, leaders, facilitators, and volunteers: you are warmly welcome. May this camp inspire you to run faithfully, serve passionately, and live victoriously for Christ.

Together, let us Run To Win! TUTUTUTUTUTU VROOOOOOOOOM!

With love and prayers,
Ps. Enoch Bortey Offei
National Youth Leader, The Apostolic Church – Ghana

Apostolic Exhortation

Message from Superintendent – Aps. Andrews Nortey

From: Aps. Andrews Nortey (Superintendent) • Theme Focus: 1 Corinthians 9:24–26; 2 Timothy 2:22

Grace, peace and mercy from the God who called us, liberated us, and gathered us as His people, be multiplied unto you, now and forever. His divine power has given unto us all things that pertain to life and godliness, and I pray that we will all become partakers of all these blessings in the here and now.

Our theme for the camp this year seeks to sharpen our focus towards our purpose as a people called, sanctified and sent. “Run to Win” (1 Cor. 9:24-26) suggests the possibility of running for the sake of participation or for the fun of it. It also suggests the possibility of failure. But the apostle's exhortation is that members of the Corinthian church should run with the aim of winning. That, he expects and demands, should be their only objective.

In the race of life, rules rule the game. The apostle indicated that those who win, run according to the rules of the game. He does not box and throw punches anyhow just to feel sweaty, but aims well and combats strategically: “Run in such a way that you may obtain it.” For this reason, Paul exhorts the church and reminds them that the one who runs to win “goes into strict training” (1 Cor. 9:25, NIV) so that they will be in the position to win the race. These are rules that cannot be broken.

How then can believers, especially young ones, ensure that they will not fail in the race they have begun? We are very much aware that though some things are lawful, not all of them are helpful. Paul again admonishes, “flee youthful lust” (2 Tim. 2:22), “let no man despise your youth” (1 Tim. 4:12), “hold on firm to the faith” (2 Thess. 2:15), then “your profiting may appear unto all” (1 Tim. 4:15).

The race we are enlisted in is the race of life. If we win, it is for life eternal; if we lose, it is to eternal damnation. How then will you run? This is a personal decision. Once the decision is made, others can hold your hands and walk along the way with you. So, how will you run? My admonishment to you is that you run to win.

May the God of all grace cause grace to multiply to you in every way possible, so that you can run with purpose and discipline, to win in the end. God bless you!

Retirement Honor & Tribute

Farewell Message from the Area Youth Ministry to Apostle W.S. Dantiar

Presented by: The Entire Youth Ministry of Agona Swedru Area • In Honor of: Apostle W.S. Dantiar (Area Superintendent)

On behalf of the entire Youth Ministry, we stand before you today with hearts full of immense gratitude, joy, and a touch of sadness as we mark this incredible milestone in your life and ministry. Throughout your tenure as our Area Apostle, you have been much more than an administrative leader — you have been a spiritual father, a steadfast mentor, and an unwavering champion for the younger generation.

In a world where youth often search for direction, your life and ministry have served as a clear beacon of light. You never looked down on our youth; instead, you believed in our potential, invested in our spiritual growth, and continually created space for us to serve, lead, and flourish in the Kingdom.

What we will always treasure is your wisdom and guidance, your genuine love for Christ, your unquenchable passion for the gospel, your down-to-earth teachings, and your fatherly heart and exemplary leadership.

As you step into this well-deserved season of retirement, please know that the seeds you have sown into the youth of this area will continue to bear fruit for generations to come. We promise to carry forward the torch of faith, unity, and dedication that you so faithfully modeled before us.

Thank you, Apostle, for loving us, guiding us, and giving your best to God's work. We love you, we honor you, and we wish you a happy and blessed retirement!
