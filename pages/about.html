The Vision & Theme

Theme: “Run To Win!”

“Do you not know that in a race all the runners run, but only one gets the prize? Run in such a way as to get the prize. Everyone who competes in the games goes into strict training. They do it to get a crown that will not last, but we do it to get a crown that will last forever.”
— 1 Corinthians 9:24–25 (NIV)

The National Youth Ministry (TAC-NGYN) of The Apostolic Church – Ghana brings together young believers across districts, tertiary institutions, secondary schools, and local assemblies. In Central Region Zone 1, our annual gathering unites the Agona Swedru Area and the Winneba-Apam Area in deep spiritual synergy with the Children's Ministry.

In a world marked by endless distractions and speed, we are charged not to pace aimlessly, but to run with holy focus, discipline, and endurance. We run to honor Christ Jesus and claim the eternal incorruptible prize.
