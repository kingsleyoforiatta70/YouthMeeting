Venue Directions & Registration Secretariat

Everything you need to plan your arrival at Swedru School of Business (SWESBUS): travel directions, registration fees, what to pack, and contacts for our secretariat.

Camp Location

Swedru School of Business (SWESBUS)

Town: Agona Swedru, Central Region, Ghana

Dates: Thursday, September 3 – Sunday, September 6, 2026

Check-in Time: Thursday from 3:00 PM onwards

Directions & Travel Advice:

From Accra / Kasoa: Board a commercial vehicle or trotro to Agona Swedru main station. From the station, take a local taxi or shared ride directly to the Swedru School of Business (SWESBUS) campus gate.

From Winneba / Apam: Vehicles heading through Gomoa Dawurampong or Swedru Junction connect directly into Swedru town. Alight near SWESBUS junction.

From Cape Coast / Mankessim: Board vehicles bound for Swedru via Ochiso. Inform the driver you are alighting at SWESBUS.

Essential Camper Packing Checklist

Please review this checklist before setting off from your local assembly:

Spiritual & Study Items

  • Holy Bible (ESV / NIV / KJV)
  • Notebook and blue/black pens
  • Highlighter for Scripture markings

Hostel & Dormitory Essentials

  • Bedsheet and pillowcase
  • Light blanket or cover cloth

  • Slippers for bathroom

Session Attire

  • Smart church clothing for main talks
  • Comfortable walking shoes for outreach
