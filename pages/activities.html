Camp Activities & Special Nights

From intense dawn devotions and community outreach in Oteipirow to the famous Saturday sports tournaments and bonfire night, discover everything happening at Camp 2026.

Friday • 2:00 PM

Oteipirow Community Evangelism

A key highlight of our camp is carrying the gospel into the surrounding community. On Friday afternoon, campers and ministers deploy into the township of Oteipirow for street evangelism, door-to-door visitation, tract sharing, and prayer ministrations for the sick.

Dress Code: Decent & comfortable walking wear, supportive walking shoes.
Friday • 6:30 PM

Career Symposium & Personal Branding

Christian excellence extends into our education, trades, and professional callings. Our Friday Inspirational Night features seasoned professionals and university alumni sharing insights on course selection, scholarship opportunities, career navigation, and personal integrity.

Dress Code: Rep Your School (JHS/SHS/Tertiary) OR Rep Your Jersey!
Saturday • 2:00 PM

Inter-District Fun Games & Athletics

Healthy competition and fellowship on the SWESBUS sports grounds. Districts from Agona Swedru Area and Winneba-Apam Area compete in sprint relays, inter-district football matches, volleyball, tug-of-peace, and indoor table games.

Dress Code: Sports attire, sneakers, district athletic colors.
Saturday • 6:30 PM

Addictions Seminar: Types, Recovery & Grace

An honest, confidential, and medically sound spiritual discussion addressing challenges confronting today's youth: substance abuse, gambling, digital/screen dependency, and sexual purity. Compassionate counseling and confidential prayer available.

Focus: Spiritual deliverance and counseling support.
Saturday • 9:00 PM

Camp Bonfire & Old-School Variety Night

An unforgettable evening under the open stars! Gathered around the campfire, we celebrate our heritage with classic Apostolic chorus medleys, spoken word, drama sketches, musical solos, and testimonies of God's faithfulness.

Dress Code: Old School Night (Throwback / Vintage Gospel Outfits).
Sunday • 8:00 AM

Climax Convocation & “As You Go”

The sacred high point of camp. All delegates assemble in uniform camp t-shirts for mass praise, choral ministration, presentation of sports and study awards, and the apostolic sending-forth prayer (*As You Go*) before returning to local assemblies.

Dress Code: Official “Run To Win” Camp T-Shirt.

Complete Camper Dress Code Summary

To help you pack accurately for your stay at SWESBUS, here is the approved dress code for every day and session:

Day & Session Required Dress Code Special Guidelines
Thursday Arrival (3:00 PM) Travel Wear Smart, modest, comfortable for travel and hostel check-in.
Thursday Evening Service (6:00 PM) Opening Service Attire Decent church clothing.
Friday Dawn & Morning Talks Smart Youth Attire Decent and comfortable for lecture sessions and discussions.
Friday Outreach (2:00 PM) Outreach Attire Comfortable walking clothes and sturdy shoes for field evangelism.
Friday Career Night (6:30 PM) Rep Your School / Rep Your Jersey School uniform, institution polo, tertiary wear, or football jersey.
Saturday Morning Talks Smart Youth Attire Modest and comfortable.
Saturday Games (2:00 PM) Sports / Casual Wear Tracksuits, jerseys, sneakers, athletic shorts with tights underneath.
Saturday Bonfire (6:30 PM & 9:00 PM) Old School / Throwback Wear Retro styles, vintage church attire, 80s/90s vintage clothing.
Sunday Climax Service (8:00 AM) Official “Run To Win” T-Shirt Official camp t-shirt worn with dark trousers or modest skirt.
