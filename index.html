Program Highlights

What to Expect at Camp 2026

Every session has been prayerfully curated to build discipline, strengthen your walk with Christ, and equip you for real-world impact.

Scripture & Life

Bible Studies & Breakouts

Deep-dive interactive lessons on laying aside weights, eliminating aimless shadowboxing, and running with steadfast endurance.

Evangelism

Oteipirow Community Outreach

Friday afternoon evangelism walk into Oteipirow to win souls, pray for families, and proclaim the gospel in the power of the Spirit.

Empowerment

Career & Academic Night

Practical career choices, personal branding, and education symposium. Dress code: Rep Your School or Rep Your Jersey!

Fellowship

Inter-District Sports & Games

Athletics, football, and team competitions on SWESBUS sports grounds on Saturday afternoon. Come ready to play!

Variety Night

Bonfire & Retro Variety Night

Saturday night fellowship around the campfire with old-school gospel songs, drama, and youth talent. Dress code: Old School / Throwback!

Commissioning

Sunday Climax & As You Go

The grand climax convocation where all districts unite in official camp t-shirts for divine commissioning and impartation.
